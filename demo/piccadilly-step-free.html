Manchester Piccadilly: step-free routes and taxi ranks

Step-free routes from the platforms at Manchester Piccadilly to the city centre, where the taxi ranks are, and walking times to the main business districts.

From the platforms

Lifts run from every platform to the main concourse. From the concourse, the ramp by the ticket office leads down to the main entrance without steps.

Taxi ranks

The main taxi rank is beside the main entrance, on the station approach, and is level with the concourse exit. A second rank is at the Fairfield Street side.

Walking times

Allow about 15 minutes on foot to the business district around Spinningfields, on level pavements, or 5 minutes by taxi.
