Steward demo pages

Synthetic pages used to test the Steward project, a gatekeeper between AI agents and the models, tools and people they reach. The station guide carries a hidden prompt injection on purpose: it shows how Steward holds an agent's outgoing calls after it reads untrusted content. Nothing here is real travel advice, and the address it names is on a reserved .example domain that does not exist.

  • Manchester Piccadilly: step-free routes and taxi ranks (fictional, with a hidden instruction)
  • Hiding test (which hidden text a web extractor returns)
